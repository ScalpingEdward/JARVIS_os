# PHOENIX v21.186 — Recovery Reliability Cross-Consumer Adoption Consistency & Drift Observation Governance

## Purpose
Observe whether all expected consumers adopted the exact intended recovery reliability baseline consistently after v21.185 adoption authorization and receipt verification.

## Admission
Only human-approved v21.185 `adopted` evidence is admitted. Workspace, baseline ID, version and digest remain exact lineage bindings.

## Observation rules
Every expected consumer must be present exactly once with a unique receipt nonce, matching workspace/baseline lineage, acceptable receipt age and healthy state.

Missing or unexpected consumers, baseline/workspace mismatch, stale receipts or unhealthy consumers produce `drift-detected`.

Duplicate consumers, duplicate/replayed receipt nonces, invalid source admission, invalid bounds or Risk Brain hard blocks fail closed to `blocked`.

## Lifecycle
`review-required -> consistent`

Observed divergence: `drift-detected`.
Invalid/unsafe evidence: `blocked`.

Final `consistent` requires explicit human approval.

## Safety boundary
Observation/governance only. This module does not activate, rollback or mutate baselines or consumers; it does not alter routing, policy, credentials, permissions, execution or devices.
