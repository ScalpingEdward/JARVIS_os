# PHOENIX v21.195 — Recovery Reliability Baseline Adoption Authorization & Receipt Governance

## Purpose
Authorize a staged reliability-baseline adoption for one consumer and reconcile fresh evidence that the intended baseline was adopted.

## Admission
Only human-approved v21.194 `staged` evidence is admitted. Workspace, consumer, baseline ID/version/digest and rollback lineage remain bound to the record.

## Lifecycle
`review-required -> authorized -> receipt-required -> adopted`

Invalid lineage, stale evidence, replayed nonces, failed authorization or Risk Brain hard blocks fail closed to `blocked`.

## Receipt requirements
A receipt must match the exact consumer/workspace/baseline lineage, report `adopted=true`, include a unique nonce and remain within the configured evidence TTL.

## Safety boundary
Governance/evidence reconciliation only. This module does not itself activate a baseline, mutate runtime consumers, change routing or policy, expand permissions or credentials, submit trades, move funds or actuate devices.
