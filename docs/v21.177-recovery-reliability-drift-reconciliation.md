# PHOENIX v21.177 — Recovery Reliability Adoption Drift Escalation & Coordinated Reconciliation Readiness Governance

## Purpose
Escalate cross-consumer recovery reliability adoption drift into a bounded, auditable reconciliation-readiness plan.

## Admission
Only human-approved v21.176 `drift-detected` evidence is admitted. Workspace and exact baseline ID/version/digest lineage remain bound.

## Planning model
The record separates affected consumers from healthy consumers and binds one or more drift findings to every affected consumer. Healthy consumers are preserved as non-targets for later reconciliation.

Readiness is derived from drift severity, blast radius and residual risk. Configurable maximum blast-radius/residual-risk guardrails fail closed when exceeded.

## Lifecycle
`review-required -> reconciliation-ready`

Invalid lineage, consumer overlap, missing drift findings, replayed source evidence, invalid metrics or Risk Brain hard blocks fail closed to `blocked`.

Final `reconciliation-ready` requires explicit human approval and the deterministic readiness score to meet the configured threshold.

## Safety boundary
Planning/governance only. This module does not change consumers, activate or roll back baselines, mutate routing/policy, expand credentials/permissions, submit trades, move funds or actuate devices.
