# PHOENIX v21.174 — Recovery Reliability Baseline Controlled Rollout & Adoption Eligibility Governance

## Purpose
Govern staged rollout eligibility for a human-approved committed recovery reliability baseline while preserving exact rollback lineage.

## Admission
Only v21.173 `committed` evidence with human approval is accepted. Workspace, baseline ID/version/digest and rollback version/value remain bound to the rollout record.

## Lifecycle
`review-required -> eligible -> staged`

Any invalid source, missing binding, bad rollback lineage, duplicate/overlapping consumer assignment, stage-order violation, missing human approval or Risk Brain hard block fails closed to `blocked`.

## Human approval
Separate explicit approval is required for rollout eligibility and each rollout stage. A later stage cannot be approved before all earlier stages.

## Safety boundary
This module records rollout eligibility only. It does not activate the baseline, mutate runtime consumers, change routing/policy, expand permissions/credentials, execute trades, move funds or actuate devices.
