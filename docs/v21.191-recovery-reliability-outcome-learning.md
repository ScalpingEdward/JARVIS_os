# PHOENIX v21.191 — Recovery Reliability Outcome Learning & Baseline Feedback Governance

## Purpose
Convert a human-approved, closed recovery episode into bounded learning evidence for the next baseline-feedback simulation cycle.

## Admission
Only human-approved v21.190 `closed` evidence is admitted. Workspace and baseline ID/version/digest lineage remain exact.

## Learning evidence
The module binds:
- stability score
- aggregate confidence
- recovery quality
- residual risk
- proposed bounded feedback adjustment

All normalized evidence must remain inside `[0, 1]`. Proposed feedback is limited to an absolute adjustment of `0.05`.

Weak stability, confidence or recovery quality, excessive residual risk, duplicate source evidence, invalid lineage/source admission or a Risk Brain hard block fail closed to `blocked`.

## Lifecycle
`review-required -> approved-feedback`

Final feedback eligibility requires explicit human approval.

## Safety boundary
This module produces learning/governance evidence only. It does not modify committed or active baselines, change runtime consumers, alter routing/policy/credentials/permissions, submit trades, move funds or actuate devices.
