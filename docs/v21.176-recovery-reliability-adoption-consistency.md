# PHOENIX v21.176 — Recovery Reliability Cross-Consumer Adoption Consistency & Drift Observation Governance

## Purpose
Verify that every expected consumer has adopted the exact intended recovery reliability baseline and remains healthy after adoption.

## Admission
Only human-approved v21.175 `adopted` evidence is admitted. Workspace, baseline ID, version and digest remain exact lineage bindings.

## Observation
Each expected consumer must provide unique adoption evidence matching:
- consumer identity
- workspace
- baseline ID
- baseline version
- baseline digest
- receipt digest
- healthy state

The module calculates a deterministic cross-consumer consistency score and records drift reasons.

## Lifecycle
`review-required -> consistent`

Evidence mismatches, missing or unhealthy consumers produce `drift-detected`.
Invalid source admission, duplicate/replayed receipts, duplicate consumers or Risk Brain hard blocks fail closed to `blocked`.

Final `consistent` requires explicit human approval.

## Safety boundary
Observation/governance only. It never mutates consumers, activates baselines, changes routing or policy, expands credentials/permissions, submits orders, moves funds or actuates devices.
