# PHOENIX v21.170 — Reliability Recovery Stability Observation & Episode Closure Governance

## Purpose
Observe the recovered consumer set after v21.169 completion and close the recovery episode only when the exact intended baseline remains stable.

## Admission
Only human-approved `completed` recovery reconciliation evidence may enter. Workspace, baseline ID, version and digest remain exact lineage bindings.

## Observation
Each expected consumer is evaluated for health, exact baseline match, dependency satisfaction, latency quality, error quality, confidence and freshness.

The module computes deterministic aggregate stability and residual-risk scores. Missing consumers, invalid metric ranges, invalid lineage/source admission or Risk Brain hard blocks fail closed to `blocked`. Baseline drift or excessive residual risk produces `degraded`.

## Lifecycle
`review-required -> closed`

Degraded evidence remains `degraded`; invalid/unsafe evidence becomes `blocked`.

Final episode closure requires explicit human approval.

## Safety boundary
Observation/governance only. No consumer, baseline, route, policy, credential, permission, trade, fund movement or device is autonomously mutated.
