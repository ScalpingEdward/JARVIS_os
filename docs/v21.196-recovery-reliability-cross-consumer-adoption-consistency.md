# PHOENIX v21.196 — Recovery Reliability Cross-Consumer Adoption Consistency & Drift Observation Governance

## Purpose
Validate that all expected consumers adopted the same recovery reliability baseline with fresh, exact and healthy evidence before the system treats the rollout as consistent.

## Admission
Only human-approved upstream records in `adopted` state are admissible.

## Evidence checks
- exact workspace match
- exact baseline ID/version/digest match
- complete expected-consumer coverage
- no unexpected consumers
- unique consumer IDs
- unique receipt nonces
- receipt freshness within configured TTL
- `adopted=true`
- `healthy=true`
- confidence within `[0.0, 1.0]`

## Outcomes
- clean evidence remains `review-required` until explicit human approval
- approved clean evidence becomes `consistent`
- missing, stale, mismatched, unhealthy or non-adopted evidence becomes `drift-detected`
- invalid source, replay/duplicate nonce, malformed confidence or Risk Brain block becomes fail-closed `blocked`

## Safety boundary
Observation and governance only. This module does not activate or rollback a baseline, mutate a runtime consumer, move funds, submit orders, change routing, alter credentials/permissions or actuate devices.
