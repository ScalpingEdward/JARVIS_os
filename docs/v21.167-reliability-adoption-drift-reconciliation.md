# PHOENIX v21.167 — Reliability Adoption Drift Escalation & Coordinated Reconciliation Readiness Governance

## Purpose
Convert v21.166 `drift-detected` evidence into a bounded, explicit, human-reviewed coordinated reconciliation-readiness plan.

## Admission
Only cross-consumer adoption evidence in `drift-detected` state may enter. Workspace and baseline ID/version/digest lineage remain exact and immutable inside the governance record.

## Evaluation
The module separates affected and healthy consumers, binds each affected consumer to a drift reason and expected baseline identity, and evaluates consistency score, blast radius and residual risk against configured limits.

## Lifecycle
`review-required -> reconciliation-ready`

Any invalid source, missing binding, consumer overlap, duplicate affected consumer, baseline mismatch, excessive blast radius, excessive residual risk or Risk Brain hard block fails closed to `blocked`.

## Safety boundary
`reconciliation-ready` is planning evidence only. No consumer, baseline, route, policy, credential, permission or execution setting is changed.
