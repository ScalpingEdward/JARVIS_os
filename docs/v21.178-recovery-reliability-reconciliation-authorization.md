# PHOENIX v21.178 — Recovery Reliability Reconciliation Authorization & Ordered Consumer Recovery Governance

## Purpose
Authorize and sequence consumer recovery after a human-approved reconciliation-ready drift plan.

## Admission
Only human-approved v21.177 `reconciliation-ready` evidence is admitted. Workspace and exact baseline ID/version/digest lineage remain bound.

## Lifecycle
`review-required -> authorized -> staged -> recovery-ready`

Any invalid source, affected/healthy overlap, malformed sequence, risk-limit violation, missing human approval or Risk Brain hard block fails closed to `blocked`.

## Ordered recovery
Every affected consumer must appear exactly once in the deterministic recovery sequence. Healthy consumers are explicitly preserved. Recovery steps must be approved in order; skipping ahead fails closed.

## Safety boundary
This module authorizes governance steps only. It does not mutate runtime consumers, activate or roll back a baseline, change routing/policy, expand credentials/permissions, submit trades, move funds or actuate devices.
