# PHOENIX v21.164 — Reliability Baseline Controlled Rollout & Adoption Eligibility Governance

## Purpose
Govern downstream eligibility and ordered rollout stages for a human-approved committed reliability baseline.

## Admission
Only a v21.163 source in `committed` state with human approval may enter. Workspace, baseline ID, version and digest remain bound to the rollout record.

## Lifecycle
`review-required -> eligible -> staged`

Any invalid source, missing binding, duplicate consumer, stage overlap/order violation, missing human approval or Risk Brain hard block fails closed to `blocked`.

## Safety boundary
This module records eligibility and human-approved rollout stages only. It does not activate a baseline, mutate a runtime consumer, change routing or policy, expand permissions/credentials, move funds or submit orders.

## Adoption rule
A staged consumer is merely eligible for a later explicit adoption workflow. Adoption must produce separately verified receipts before consistency can be claimed.
