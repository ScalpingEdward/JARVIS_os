# PHOENIX v21.184 — Recovery Reliability Baseline Controlled Rollout & Adoption Eligibility Governance

## Purpose
Govern controlled downstream eligibility for the next recovery-reliability baseline cycle after v21.183.

## Admission
Only a human-approved v21.183 source in `committed` state is admitted. Workspace, baseline ID/version/digest and rollback binding remain exact.

## Additional v21.184 guardrail
Unlike the earlier rollout cycle, v21.184 adds a bounded per-stage exposure fraction. A rollout stage may not contain more than the configured share of total candidate consumers.

## Lifecycle
`review-required -> eligible -> staged`

Invalid lineage, missing approval, duplicate/overlapping consumers, unordered stages, excessive stage exposure or a Risk Brain hard block fail closed to `blocked`.

## Safety boundary
This module records rollout eligibility and human-approved stage progression only. It does not activate a baseline, mutate runtime consumers, change routing/policy, expand permissions/credentials, submit trades, move funds or actuate devices.
