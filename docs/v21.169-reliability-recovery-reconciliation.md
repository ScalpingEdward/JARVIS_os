# PHOENIX v21.169 — Reliability Recovery Receipt Reconciliation & Cross-Consumer Completion Governance

## Purpose
Close an ordered reliability recovery only after fresh evidence proves every expected affected consumer recovered onto the exact intended baseline.

## Admission
Only human-approved v21.168 `recovery-ready` evidence is admitted. Workspace, baseline ID, version and digest remain exact lineage bindings.

## Evidence
Each expected consumer must provide one fresh recovery receipt containing consumer/workspace identity, exact baseline ID/version/digest, a unique nonce and positive recovered state.

Missing or mismatched receipts produce `incomplete`. Duplicate/replayed nonces, invalid lineage/source admission or Risk Brain hard blocks fail closed to `blocked`.

## Lifecycle
`review-required -> completed`

Evidence gaps: `incomplete`.
Invalid or unsafe admission: `blocked`.

Final completion requires explicit human approval.

## Safety boundary
This module reconciles evidence only. It does not mutate consumers, activate baselines, change routing/policy, expand permissions/credentials, submit trades, move funds or actuate devices.
