# PHOENIX v21.187 — Recovery Reliability Adoption Drift Escalation & Coordinated Reconciliation Readiness Governance

## Purpose
Escalate cross-consumer adoption drift from v21.186 into a bounded, reviewable reconciliation-readiness decision.

## Admission
Only human-approved `drift-detected` evidence is admitted. Workspace and exact baseline ID/version/digest lineage remain authoritative.

## Evaluation
The module binds affected consumers to drift reason and severity, preserves healthy consumers separately, rejects overlap/duplicates, computes a deterministic readiness score, and applies explicit blast-radius and residual-risk guardrails.

## Lifecycle
`review-required -> reconciliation-ready`

Invalid lineage, excessive blast radius, excessive residual risk, insufficient readiness, duplicate source evidence, consumer overlap, or a Risk Brain hard block fails closed to `blocked`.

## Safety boundary
Planning/governance only. `reconciliation-ready` means a bounded recovery plan may enter the next authorization workflow. No consumer, baseline, routing, policy, credential, permission, trade, fund, device, or runtime state is mutated here.
