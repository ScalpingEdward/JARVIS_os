# PHOENIX v21.189 — Recovery Reliability Recovery Receipt Reconciliation & Cross-Consumer Completion Governance

## Purpose
Close an ordered recovery sequence only after fresh, exact, healthy recovery evidence proves that every expected consumer recovered onto the intended baseline.

## Admission
Only human-approved v21.188 `recovery-ready` evidence is admitted. Workspace, baseline ID, version and digest remain exact lineage bindings.

## Evidence requirements
Each expected consumer must provide one unique recovery receipt containing:
- consumer/workspace identity
- exact baseline ID/version/digest
- unique recovery nonce
- positive recovered + healthy state
- bounded recovery quality score
- evidence age within the configured TTL

Missing, stale, low-quality or mismatched receipts produce `incomplete`. Invalid source admission, duplicate/replayed nonces, malformed lineage or Risk Brain hard blocks fail closed to `blocked`.

## Lifecycle
`review-required -> completed`

Evidence gaps: `incomplete`.
Unsafe or invalid admission: `blocked`.

Final completion requires explicit human approval after a perfect reconciliation score.

## Safety boundary
Evidence reconciliation only. This module does not mutate consumers, activate or roll back baselines, change routing/policy, expand permissions/credentials, submit trades, move funds or actuate devices.
