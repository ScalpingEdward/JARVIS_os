# PHOENIX v21.188 — Recovery Reliability Reconciliation Authorization & Ordered Consumer Recovery Governance

## Purpose
Authorize an exact, bounded and ordered consumer-recovery plan after v21.187 declares reconciliation readiness.

## Admission
Only human-approved `reconciliation-ready` evidence is admitted. Workspace, baseline ID, version and digest remain immutable lineage bindings.

## Recovery ordering
Every affected consumer appears exactly once in the ordered recovery sequence. Healthy consumers are excluded from the recovery set. Every recovery step binds the originating drift reason and requires explicit human approval in sequence.

## Guardrails
- blast radius must remain within the configured bound
- residual risk must remain within the configured bound
- affected and healthy consumer sets must not overlap
- recovery order must be contiguous and deterministic
- Risk Brain blocks are authoritative
- duplicate source evidence fails closed

## Lifecycle
`review-required -> authorized -> staged -> recovery-ready`

Any invalid admission, out-of-order approval, risk-limit breach or Risk Brain hard block produces `blocked`.

## Safety boundary
Authorization/governance only. `recovery-ready` means the exact ordered plan has been human-authorized step by step. This module does not mutate consumers, activate or roll back baselines, change routing/policy/credentials/permissions, submit trades, move funds or actuate devices.
