# PHOENIX v21.179 — Recovery Reliability Recovery Receipt Reconciliation & Cross-Consumer Completion Governance

## Purpose
Close the ordered recovery-reliability reconciliation only after fresh evidence proves every expected affected consumer recovered onto the exact intended baseline and meets the minimum recovery-quality threshold.

## Admission
Only human-approved v21.178 `recovery-ready` evidence is admitted. Workspace, baseline ID, version and digest remain exact lineage bindings.

## Evidence
Every expected consumer must provide one fresh recovery receipt containing consumer/workspace identity, exact baseline ID/version/digest, a unique nonce, positive recovered/healthy state, bounded recovery score and evidence digest.

Missing or mismatched receipts produce `incomplete`. Duplicate/replayed nonces, invalid lineage/source admission or Risk Brain hard blocks fail closed to `blocked`.

## Lifecycle
`review-required -> completed`

Evidence gaps: `incomplete`.
Invalid/unsafe admission: `blocked`.
Final completion requires explicit human approval.

## Safety boundary
Evidence reconciliation only. This module does not mutate consumers, activate/rollback baselines, change routing/policy, expand permissions/credentials, submit trades, move funds or actuate devices.
